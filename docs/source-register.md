# Verified Source Register

**Last checked:** 2026-08-11  
**Scope:** sources used to support the product, international benchmark, web
delivery plan, and architecture blueprint  
**Important:** this register is a dated research record, not a substitute for a
fresh point-in-time legal check before giving advice.

## 1. Source acceptance policy

- Prefer a signed original or Electronic Official Gazette copy for controlling
  text and the National Legal Database for metadata, history, related documents,
  and consolidated views.
- Record both canonical URL and stored artifact hash. Web content may change.
- Verify identifier, issuer, promulgation date, effective date, status, and the
  precise cited provision.
- A consolidated document is useful for reading but must be traceable to every
  original and amendment.
- Official agency explanation is not automatically a normative instrument.
- Papers and product pages support technical or market analysis only; they do
  not support a Vietnamese legal conclusion.

## 6. Verification gaps to close before implementation

1. Obtain written terms/API or data-sharing arrangements for each official and
   licensed source; no production crawler is approved by this document.
2. Commission current consolidated legal opinions for the professional-service
   boundary, AI risk classification, personal-data roles/transfers, consumer
   terms, confidentiality, and source reuse.
3. Verify every tax implementing instrument and transition at the time a domain
   is onboarded; the table is illustrative, not an exhaustive tax corpus.
4. Audit Vietnamese legal datasets for license, provenance, duplicates, temporal
   cutoff, answer leakage, and expert label quality.
5. Re-run vendor/product observations under permitted terms; public feature pages
   do not prove accuracy or security.
